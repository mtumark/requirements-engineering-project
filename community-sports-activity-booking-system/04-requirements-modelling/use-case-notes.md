## Week 4 - Requirements Modelling

## Requirement used
* The system shall allow users to check the availability of a facility for a selected date and time.

## Actors
* User / Customer
* Booking Staff

## Use Cases
* View Facility Availability
* Create Booking
* Cancel Booking
* Manage Facility Availability 

## Unknowns / Questions
* Who is allowed to book each facility?
* Do users need to log in to check availability?
* Which staff members can manage facility availability and booking rules?
* Can staff cancel or update bookings on behalf of users?

## Reflection
The diagram helped me understand who uses the system and what they can do. Some staff permissions and booking rules are still unclear and need to be confirmed.
