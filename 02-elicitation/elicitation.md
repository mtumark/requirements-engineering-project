## 1. Stakeholders
* Equipment mngr. - Manage the booking, releasing and return 
* Student - Booking and Borrowing shared equipment
* Equipment technicians - Making sure the equipments are still in good condition
* Lecturer - Booking and Borrowing shared equipment
* Custodian - Thinks about the number of equipments / stocks
* Dept. Admin - Managing the records and reports

## 2. Stakeholder needs and concerns
* Equipment mngr.
  - Possible need: Easier way to manage and handle the booking.
  - Possible concern: Equipment are double booked and not returned on time.
* Student
  - Possible need: Easy way to book an equipment and knowing its availability.
  - Possible concern: Equipment is not available when needed.
* Equipment technicians
  - Possible need: In good condition equipments
  - Possible concern: Damage or defective equipments 
* Lecturer
  - Possible need: Easy way to book an equipment and knowing its availability.
  - Possible concern: Equipment is not available when needed.
* Custodian 
  - Possible need: There are enough equipments for the school
  - Possible concern: Missing or uaccountable equipment
* Dept. Admin
  - Possible need: Proper records and analytics for the equipment
  - Possible concern: Concern about other stakeholders issue 

## 3. Unknowns
* Whats the timeframe for an equipment? - Equipment mngr.
* How do they handle overdue equipment? - Equipment mngr.
