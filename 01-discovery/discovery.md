# Week 1 - Intial Discovery

## 1. Facts
* Students and staff are having difficulty in booking equipements.
* Curently booking are managed manually, such as email, spreadsheets and informal arrangements.
* Student and staff are unaware of the equipments availability.
* Their are two main group of entities who are having the issue in booking of the equipmets.
* It consumes the time of the staff to check the availability of the equipments.

## 2. Assumptions
* They need a web system or mobile app
* Student wants to receive a notice or reminder that the equipment they borrowed is overdue.
* Staff wants to be notified if an equipment is being borrowed.
* Their is a time limit for a student to borrow.
* Its not just the student that wants to borrow the equipments.

## 3. Unknowns
* Aside from student who else are allowed to book an equipment?
* Is their a time limit for a student to borrow?
* From what time an equipment is allowed to be borrow?
* Number of students, staff and equipments

## 4. Steakholders
* Student - Availability, easy booking
* Staff - Easy equipment and booking management
* Admin - Reports
* IT - System usage
* Suppliers - Number of equipments

## 5. Goals
* Make booking easy and accessible
* Generate reports easily
* Lessen the risk of loss or damage equipments

## 6. Scope
* Should have one channel to book equipements to avoid double booking
* Booked equipments should have a timeframe

## 7. Candidate Requirements
* The system needs to have a timeframe when can an authorized user request equipment.
* The system needs to have notification function.
* The system should show the availability of equipments and if its ok to book.
* The system should also have a cancel booking function.
* Accessibility, performance, reliability, privacy
