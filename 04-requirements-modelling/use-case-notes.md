## Week 4 - Requirements Modelling

## Requirement used
* Students and lecturers are able to view available equipments, book and cancel it.

## Actors
* Student
* Lecturer

## Use Cases
* View equipment availability
* Create booking
* Cancel booking

## Unknowns / Questions
* Who will check the returned equipment?
* If student or lecturer have question, who will answer it?
* Will the system generates report, for analytics? Who will check it?

## Reflection
* It makes it easier for me to see the picture of the scope of the problem
* It revealed that their are a lot of missing informations we still need to elicit.
