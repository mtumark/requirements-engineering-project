## 3. Requirements Surgery
**Worked example: "The system shall manage bookings efficiently."**

FIND: “Efficiently” is vague.

DIAGNOSE: It is subjective and not measurable/testable.
* What does "**efficiently**" mean?
* Who is involved? Which part of the booking proccess?
* What should the system actually do?
* How would we know whether it has been satisfied?
  
ASK: What part of the booking process currently causes difficulty

REPAIR: The system shall allow users to create, view, modify, and cancel bookings and shall prevent double-booking of an unavailable time slot.

CHECK: Test each booking operation and attempt a duplicate booking to verify that the system prevents double-booking.

## 4. Functional Requirements
**Requirement 1: "The system shall be user-friendly"**
* What is the problem? 
  - “User-friendly” is vague and subjective.
* One Clarification question
  - What specific features would make the system user-friendly for its intended users?
* What information is missing?
  - The intended users, their needs, and the specific usability criteria or measurable characteristics that define “user-friendly.”

**Requirement 2: "The system shall notify users about bookings."**
* What is the problem?
  - It does not specify when, how, or which stakeholders should be notified.
* One Clarification question
  - When should users be notified about a booking, and how should they receive the notification?
* What information is missing?
  - The stakeholders who should receive the notification, the events that trigger a notification, and the notification method (e.g., email, SMS, or in-system notification).

**Requirement 3: "The system shall allow staff to create, edit and delete bookings and send reminders."**
* What is the problem?
  - It combines multiple functions and does not specify how they should work.
* One Clarification question
  - When should reminders be sent, and how should they be delivered?
* What information is missing?
  - The specific booking details staff can create, edit, or delete, and the reminder timing, recipients, and delivery method.
